Support

Email jiojioxiaoxiao@gmail.com for help with Stillpare. Please include your app version, device model and a short description of the issue. Send photos only if you want us to see them; never send passwords or payment details.

Compare photos

Add photos in Library, select any two as A and B, then tap Compare. Add photos keeps the current set; Replace current set starts a fresh comparison set. Saved Picks remain available.

Save a video

Choose two photos, open Create video, and select an effect, format and duration. Animated effect previews are free. Stillpare Pro unlocks video creation, saving and sharing.

Restore or manage Pro

Open Pro from Library and choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Manage your subscription in the app or in your device's Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. For billing help or refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support.

Privacy policy

Effective September 16, 2026. Stillpare is provided by Zhou Yang. Privacy questions: jiojioxiaoxiao@gmail.com.

Photos and local processing

Imported photos, comparison sets and Picks are stored in the app's storage on your device. Photo comparison, face framing and video rendering happen on your device. Stillpare does not upload your photos or videos to a developer server. Face framing finds areas to inspect; it does not identify people.

Permissions and sharing

Photo access lets you import selected images and, when requested, save photos or videos and create albums. Sharing sends the files you choose to your selected destination. Device backups and services you share to follow their own settings and policies.

Purchases

Apple processes payments and manages subscriptions. Stillpare uses Apple's verified purchase status to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment card details or operate a subscription-account server.

Analytics and support

The app has no ads, tracking SDK, or developer analytics service. If you email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond and resolve your request. Support email is handled through Gmail. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any retention legally required.

Control and deletion

Removing an imported photo from Stillpare does not delete the original in Apple Photos. Deleting the app removes its local library; exports saved in Apple Photos remain. You can manage photo permissions in device Settings. Device backups may retain app data according to your backup settings.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics or cookies. The hosting provider may process technical request information to serve and protect the site; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Subscriptions

Photo comparison and Picks are free. Stillpare Pro unlocks video creation and export. US plans are $4.99 per month or $19.99 per year, with the annual amount billed annually. Localized prices are shown by Apple before purchase.

Eligible new subscribers may receive one month free. After a trial, the chosen plan renews automatically at the price shown unless cancelled. Apple determines trial eligibility. Manage or cancel through your Apple Account subscriptions. Cancellation stops future renewal; access continues until the entitlement period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
